Our Philosophy
Slow
is better
"We don't do fast. We do intentional. Every drink takes the time it deserves."

Our baristas train for six months before they pull a single shot on bar. Our matcha is hand-whisked. Our food uses no shortcuts. This is what we mean when we say craft.

We believe the café is one of the last truly public spaces — a place for conversation, for focus, for rest. We protect that.

We are
Matcha House

Built on the belief that how you make something matters as much as what you make.

Matcha House started as a deliberate act of slowness in a city that never stops moving. Our founders — a barista and a tea master — wanted to prove that the craft experience didn't have to come with a plane ticket to Tokyo or Melbourne.

We started in a 200 sq ft space in 2019 with a single grinder and a bag of Ethiopian beans. Today we occupy three floors, employ 22 people, and serve over 600 guests daily — but the ethos has never changed.

Every decision at Matcha House starts with one question: does this make the experience more honest, more intentional, more alive? If the answer is no, we don't do it.
